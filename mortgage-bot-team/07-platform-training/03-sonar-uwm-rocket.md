# Sonar, UWM EASE, and Rocket Pro: training and workflow research

Research date: 2026-11-11. This is a source-backed training map for the proposed mortgage bot team, not operating instructions or proof that any bot can use these products. No account, borrower record, live loan, authenticated course, or vendor test environment was accessed. Vendor descriptions are product claims, not independent validation. Features, menus, access rights, products, lender policies, and pricing can change; verify against the active account and current lender instructions before use.

## Systems and responsibilities

- **Sonar** is a mortgage technology platform described by its vendor as combining point of sale (POS), loan origination system (LOS), customer relationship management (CRM), and product pricing engine (PPE) functions for brokers. Its enterprise **Apex** offering is an AI layer intended to work alongside an existing LOS. These are distinct offerings and configurations; do not assume a particular deployment has every feature. Sonar's privacy policy identifies Simplist Technologies, Inc. as the provider. [Sonar FAQ](https://www.yoursonar.com/faq/); [privacy policy](https://www.yoursonar.com/privacy/).
- **UWM EASE** is UWM's lender-side partner platform. It is not interchangeable with a broker's LOS, even where an integration exists. UWM's [processor workshop](https://www.uwm.com/processor-workshop) identifies EASE Docs and related processing resources; a dated [December 2024 UWM guide](https://res.cloudinary.com/uwm/image/upload/v1733227959/UWM.com/Documents/Differentiators/UWM-Differentiators-12032024.pdf) describes a bidirectional LOS/EASE interface. Current availability and integration behavior must be checked per account and LOS.
- **Rocket Pro** is Rocket Mortgage's wholesale partner offering; the former Rocket Pro TPO public URL redirects to [Rocket Pro](https://www.rocketpro.com/). Its [benefits page](https://www.rocketpro.com/benefits) describes the partner tools. **Rocket Pro Navigate** is a separate AI tool, not proof of the submission portal's functions or a broker LOS. [Rocket Pro Navigate](https://www.rocketpro.com/rocket-pro-navigate).

## Access, isolation, and test protocol

1. **Inventory actual access.** For each system, identify the brokerage tenant, licensed user's role, company permissions, allowed integrations, available synthetic training environment, and a vendor contact. UWM partner training and lender functions may require an approved broker account. Rocket's curated public courses may have different access requirements from its broker tools. Sonar requires vendor-provided deployment/onboarding access. Specific credentials and permissions have not been examined. Do not share passwords, bypass MFA or use another person's account; ask each vendor how a permitted service or test identity is provisioned and whether automated computer access is authorized.
2. **Keep data isolated.** Use a separate vendor-approved sandbox or training tenant and explicitly synthetic borrower, property, documents, emails, phone numbers and vendor contacts. Confirm that actions in that environment cannot send real messages, create real orders or charges, trigger credit/AUS, lock pricing, transmit disclosures, register a live loan, or affect production records. If the vendor cannot provide those guarantees, limit work to observed demonstrations and drafts.
3. **Establish system ownership.** For a sample transaction, identify the authoritative record for application, LOS data, lender status, AUS findings, conditions, fee figures, disclosures and delivery timestamps. Determine direction and conflict handling of each integration; do not infer synchronization merely from an integration claim.
4. **Capture auditable task evidence.** With permission and synthetic data, record the source, date/version, test account and role, initial state, inputs, visible outcome, generated artifact and whether an action actually reached its destination. Redact credentials and session tokens. Record course titles, dates, and steps observed, then ask a qualified LO or processor and lender representative to review each workflow.
5. **Test actions incrementally.** For each system, practice (a) locate a synthetic file and reconcile borrower identity; (b) identify missing synthetic documents; (c) compare a synthetic file against a provided, dated checklist and AUS findings; (d) prepare a submission package without sending; (e) map a mock condition to a document and draft a request; (f) prepare a mock fee comparison and discrepancy list; (g) draft status updates to fictitious contacts. With explicit vendor confirmation that test delivery and ordering cannot reach real parties, separately test a sandbox disclosure request, title/insurance/appraisal order, early-CD request and final-CD comparison. Capture what was accepted, rejected, or remained a draft; do not mark a condition cleared or disclosure delivered without a verified receipt/status.
6. **Promotion checks.** Before using real borrower data or granting write authority, verify vendor and brokerage authorization, data-processing/security terms, least-privilege roles, audit logs, retention, secrets management, account/session isolation, state/licensing and company policies, and whether Bot HQ's computer/browser actions enforce the intended approval gates. Evaluate accuracy on representative synthetic files, including contradictory data and missing documents, with human review. A staged draft does not establish production readiness, compliant handling, successful transmission or a lender decision.

## Remaining source and access gaps

- A current, account-specific curriculum for EASE registration, disclosures, fees, conditions, service orders, CD requests and closing; actual UWM course contents were not inspected.
- Rocket Pro University catalog and U.S. broker portal demonstrations for the same workflows; distinguish U.S. materials from similarly named Canadian Rocket Pro/Finmo materials.
- Sonar's broker training, actual configuration and integration documentation; a vendor demo or hands-on account is needed to determine enabled features.
- Current permissions, terms for automated agents, sandbox availability and whether the relevant systems can be controlled safely through Bot HQ. No vendor access, integrations, credentials, production data, outbound communications or paid orders were configured during this research.
