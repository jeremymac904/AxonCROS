## Role assignments

- **Leader:** identify platform and file, route tasks, maintain evidence and due dates, reconcile differences, escalate missing permissions and uncertain outcomes. The accountable licensed professional and company retain decisions and approval.
- **Loan officer assistant:** help with lead and application completeness, source-based discrepancy review, document needs, pricing comparisons for licensed review and borrower/realtor follow-up drafts.
- **Processing assistant:** prepare file and outstanding-items checklist, compare documents and findings, track lender submission, disclosures, title, insurance, appraisal, conditions and closing reconciliation; report actual status from the appropriate system.
- **Email assistant:** classify and draft addressed messages, route sensitive material through approved channels, track replies and follow-ups. Sending authority and recipient permissions are unverified.
- **Marketing assistant:** draft educational and relationship materials or newsletters for review, use company-approved templates and distribution lists. No claim of automated list building, newsletter delivery or platform-specific CRM feature is established here.

These assignments describe proposed responsibility, not access rights. Do not share an individual’s credentials or bypass MFA; confirm company and vendor policies for service accounts, delegated access, automation, record retention, and restricted data before any connection.

### Important ARIVE distinctions

1. Integrated pricing, loan submission and loan sync are distinct capabilities. A7 lists all three for UWM and Rocket Pro, but not integrated document submission for either. A listing is not proof that a broker is approved or configured, nor proof of direction, field coverage, successful transmission, or lender review. Reopen the lender list and integration settings when implementing.
2. A1 distinguishes personal credentials for credit/DO/LPA from company credentials that can be used by company users. A1 says each user needs an account; A8 describes the contract-processor role. These are setup facts, not authorization to issue bot credentials, impersonate a person, or connect borrower data.
3. A5 says changes to fees do not update existing disclosures, and third-party Smart Fees data should be independently verified. Preserve a versioned reconciliation and escalate fee or timing questions to the responsible professional.
4. The public A5 answer says a contingency date does not by itself create a task unless a rule is configured. Verify the actual automation and audit log; never assume a reminder exists because a date exists.
5. For USDA, A5 says GUS runs outside ARIVE and findings are uploaded. Verify the current lender process, permissions and current product configuration before acting.
